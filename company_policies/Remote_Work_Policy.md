# Remote Work Policy

Our remote work policy enables flexibility while ensuring productivity and accountability. Employees may work remotely with prior approval from their direct manager and must adhere to agreed-upon schedules and deadlines.

Remote employees are required to be available during core business hours, typically 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM. Attendance at virtual meetings, prompt communication, and accessibility through designated platforms are essential to successful remote work.

All remote work arrangements must ensure a secure working environment. This includes using company-approved devices, maintaining a strong internet connection, and adhering to cybersecurity protocols. Sensitive data must be protected at all times.

Remote workers are encouraged to create a dedicated workspace to minimize distractions and maintain work-life balance. The company may provide equipment or reimburse certain expenses upon approval.

Regular performance evaluations and check-ins will ensure goals are being met. Managers will provide support and resources as needed to help remote employees succeed in their roles.
