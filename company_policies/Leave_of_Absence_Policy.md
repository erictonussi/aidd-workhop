# Leave of Absence Policy

Employees may request a leave of absence for personal, medical, or family-related reasons. All requests must be submitted in writing to Human Resources at least two weeks in advance, except in emergency situations.

Leave types include parental leave, medical leave, bereavement leave, and unpaid personal leave. Each type has specific eligibility requirements and maximum duration limits as outlined in the employee handbook.

During leave, employees are encouraged to maintain occasional communication with their manager to facilitate a smooth transition back to work. However, they are not expected to perform work duties unless on partial leave.

The company may require documentation for certain types of leave, such as a physician’s note for medical leave. Health benefits may continue during the leave period, depending on the type and length of leave.

Managers and HR will coordinate coverage of duties during an employee's absence. We are committed to supporting our team members in balancing work and personal responsibilities while ensuring business continuity.
