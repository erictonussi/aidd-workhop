# Workplace Safety Policy

Our top priority is ensuring the safety and well-being of all employees. Safety procedures must be followed at all times, and any unsafe conditions should be reported immediately to a supervisor or the safety officer.

Employees must complete mandatory safety training and wear appropriate protective equipment when required. Equipment should be operated only by trained personnel, and emergency exits must be kept clear at all times.

In the event of an accident or injury, medical assistance should be sought immediately, and an incident report must be filed with HR within 24 hours. First aid kits and fire extinguishers are located throughout the facility.

Regular safety drills will be conducted, including fire, earthquake, and lockdown drills. Participation is mandatory, and feedback from these exercises will help improve emergency response plans.

We are all responsible for maintaining a safe work environment. By following safety protocols and reporting concerns, we protect ourselves, our coworkers, and the future of the company.
