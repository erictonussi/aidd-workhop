# Social Media Policy

Employees are encouraged to use social media responsibly and professionally. Personal opinions should not be mistaken for official company statements, and posts should not include confidential information or trade secrets.

When referencing the company on personal accounts, employees must identify their views as their own. Misrepresentation of the company’s views or affiliations is prohibited and may result in disciplinary action.

Posts that contain discriminatory, defamatory, or offensive content will not be tolerated. Such behavior can harm our reputation and create an uncomfortable work environment.

Use of social media during working hours should be limited to professional purposes. Company social media accounts must be managed by authorized personnel and follow brand guidelines and communication standards.

The company reserves the right to monitor public social media activity related to its business. Employees found violating this policy may be subject to corrective action, up to and including termination.
