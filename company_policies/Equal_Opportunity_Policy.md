# Equal Opportunity Policy

We are committed to providing equal opportunities for all employees and applicants. Our hiring, promotion, and compensation practices are based on merit, qualifications, and business needs.

Discrimination or harassment based on race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, or any other protected characteristic is not tolerated. We strive to create a work environment that embraces diversity and inclusion.

All employment-related decisions are made in compliance with applicable laws and are aimed at fostering a culture of fairness and equity. Training on diversity and inclusion is mandatory for all managers.

Employees who feel they have been discriminated against or harassed should report the incident to HR immediately. All complaints will be investigated promptly and confidentially, and appropriate corrective action will be taken.

We believe that a diverse workforce is a strong workforce. We are committed to continuous improvement in our diversity and inclusion efforts through open dialogue and active support of employee resource groups.
