# IT Security Policy

Information security is critical to our operations. All employees must use complex, unique passwords and change them regularly. Password sharing is strictly prohibited, and multi-factor authentication is encouraged.

Company devices must be locked when unattended, and unauthorized software or external devices may not be installed or used without approval from IT. Data must be backed up regularly, and any suspected breach should be reported immediately.

Employees must complete annual cybersecurity training and stay informed about phishing scams and other common threats. Secure browsing practices and caution when opening attachments or clicking links are vital.

Access to sensitive information is granted on a need-to-know basis. Only authorized personnel should have access to critical systems, and audit logs must be maintained to track system activity.

Failure to follow this policy may result in disciplinary action. Protecting company data is a shared responsibility, and adherence to these guidelines ensures a secure digital workplace for all.
