# Expense Reimbursement Policy

Employees may be reimbursed for business-related expenses such as travel, meals, lodging, and office supplies. All expenses must be approved by a manager and submitted within 30 days of the transaction.

Receipts are required for all expenses over $25. Claims must be submitted using the designated reimbursement form, with accurate descriptions and appropriate category tags. Incomplete submissions may be delayed or rejected.

Certain expenses, such as alcohol or first-class travel, are not eligible for reimbursement unless pre-approved. When in doubt, employees should consult with their supervisor before incurring costs.

Reimbursements will be processed biweekly through payroll. Employees are responsible for keeping personal and company expenses separate and using company credit cards only for authorized purchases.

Managers are responsible for reviewing and approving expense reports in a timely manner. Misuse or fraudulent claims will be investigated and may lead to disciplinary action.
