# Data Privacy Policy

We are committed to safeguarding the personal data of our employees, clients, and partners. Personal data is collected and processed only for legitimate business purposes and in accordance with applicable privacy laws.

Employees with access to sensitive data must handle it with the utmost care. Data should be stored securely, encrypted when necessary, and shared only with authorized personnel. Unnecessary duplication or retention of data is discouraged.

Data breaches or suspected misuse must be reported immediately to the Data Protection Officer. Timely response and transparency are critical in mitigating the impact of any breach.

Employees must complete annual data privacy training. Awareness of privacy principles such as data minimization, purpose limitation, and individual consent helps ensure compliance and ethical data use.

Failure to adhere to privacy policies may result in disciplinary measures, including legal consequences. Our reputation and customer trust depend on how we handle their data.
