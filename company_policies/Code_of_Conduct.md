# Code of Conduct

At our company, we uphold the highest standards of ethical behavior. Employees are expected to act with integrity, honesty, and transparency in all business dealings. This includes interactions with colleagues, customers, partners, and the broader community.

We believe in treating everyone with respect and dignity. Discrimination, harassment, bullying, or any form of intimidation is strictly prohibited and will lead to disciplinary action. We are committed to creating a safe and inclusive workplace for all.

Conflicts of interest must be avoided. Employees are required to disclose any personal or financial interests that may influence their professional decisions or compromise the company’s interests. Transparency is key to maintaining trust.

Professionalism must be maintained at all times. This includes punctuality, appropriate attire, and respectful communication. Employees are also expected to use company resources responsibly and adhere to established guidelines.

Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in corrective measures, up to and including termination. We encourage open dialogue and reporting of unethical behavior through our anonymous whistleblower system.
