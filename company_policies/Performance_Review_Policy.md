# Performance Review Policy

Performance reviews are conducted annually to assess employee contributions, align goals, and identify development opportunities. Reviews are based on key performance indicators (KPIs), peer feedback, and manager evaluations.

Employees are encouraged to actively participate in the review process. This includes self-assessment, setting new objectives, and providing feedback to their manager. Open dialogue is crucial for mutual growth.

Reviews influence decisions regarding promotions, bonuses, and salary adjustments. Consistent high performance may be rewarded, while underperformance will be addressed through improvement plans and mentoring.

All performance data is confidential and stored securely. HR and management work together to ensure that reviews are fair, unbiased, and based on measurable outcomes.

Continuous feedback throughout the year is encouraged. Managers should conduct regular check-ins and provide guidance to help employees stay on track and achieve their professional goals.
